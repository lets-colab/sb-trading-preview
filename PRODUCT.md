# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Overseas B2B buyers: procurement managers, importers and manufacturers sourcing bamboo, banana fibre yarn and banana bark and leaves from Bangladesh. They arrive cold or from a referral, often on a phone, and decide within seconds whether SB Trading looks like a serious exporter worth contacting. Their job is to find the right material, understand what is on offer, and start a quotation or order conversation with as little friction as possible. Many are not native English speakers.

## Product Purpose
SB Trading's website is a digital showroom for its natural materials: three families (bamboo, banana fibre yarn, banana bark and leaves) and nine products. It exists to turn a buyer's first impression into a real conversation.

Success is a buyer talking with **Rifat, SB Trading's AI assistant**. Rifat answers questions about the materials, gathers the buyer's requirements (product, quantity, destination, company, contact) and sets up a quotation enquiry or order request for SB Trading's team to confirm. Rifat always presents itself as an AI assistant. It never confirms prices, stock, lead times or orders on its own; those commitments come from a person at SB Trading. The page must work in full without Rifat: the catalogue and a plain enquiry form remain available.

## Brand Personality
Bold, cinematic, premium. Natural materials shown with the drama and craft usually reserved for luxury goods: real texture, confident motion, a single continuous scroll story (Arrive, Discover, Transition, Explore, Enquire). The voice is assured and plain-spoken. It says what SB Trading actually offers and claims nothing it cannot prove. The emotional goal is "these people are serious, and I want to talk to them", not "nice website".

## Anti-references
- **Sci-fi HUD and fake data:** holographic telemetry, "live" counters, invented specifications, certifications or lead times (as in the early AI-generated drafts).
- **Generic B2B marketplace:** Alibaba-style product walls, badge clutter, stock-photo grids.
- **Eco-cliché:** leaf icons, kraft-paper beige, generic "green" stock imagery.
- **Corporate template:** SaaS hero, three feature cards, a stats strip; anything that could belong to another company with the logo swapped.

## Design Principles
1. **Truth before spectacle.** Every claim, spec, photo and product name is SB Trading's own and confirmed; anything unconfirmed is labelled. Drama never covers for missing facts.
2. **The material is the hero.** Real bamboo, fibre and bark carry the story; motion, 3D and sound exist to make texture felt, never to decorate.
3. **One thread from first frame to first message.** The cinematic journey always leads somewhere: every chapter moves the buyer closer to talking with Rifat or sending an enquiry.
4. **Bold, then out of the way.** The opening may be big and immersive; once a buyer is looking for a product or typing a question, the interface becomes fast, quiet and clear.
5. **An honest assistant.** Rifat is clearly an AI, hands commitments to people, and degrades gracefully to a plain form when unavailable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Every immersive layer (scroll film, 3D grove, sound, smooth scrolling) has a reduced-motion and no-JavaScript equivalent; sound is off until the visitor turns it on. Rifat's conversation must be keyboard operable, screen-reader announced (live region), and never the only route to an enquiry. Plain English for non-native readers; no information carried by colour alone.
