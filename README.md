# sb-trading-preview
SB Trading — Materials in Motion | client review draft (non-production, `noindex`).

- `index.html`, `styles.css`, `site.js`: one continuous blue-hour scroll story in five stages (Arrive → Discover → Transition → Explore → Enquire), with a chapter bar along the bottom.
- `src/world.js` (bundled with three.js to `assets/vendor/world.js` by `build.mjs`): the film, a real-time 3D journey that the scroll moves the camera through, after the owner's showroom reference clip (`gemini_generated_video_97f87dc2`): a dark bamboo grove with light shafts and drifting dust; out into a round white showroom under a skylight; a bamboo pole that splits open on its pedestal; a banana stem whose fibres peel, twist and wind into a yarn ball (SB Trading's yarn is banana fibre, never bamboo); banana bark and leaves turning in gold and blue light threads; three lit glass cases. All drawn by code and labelled on the page as a 3D illustration, not SB Trading's own; SB Trading's photographs stay in Explore and the product drawers. Drawn only while the film is on screen; a lighter scene on phones and software renderers. Reduced motion, no WebGL or a lost context: the poster (`assets/img/journey-poster.webp`, a still of the grove) with the captions. `tools/build-poster.mjs` renders the poster and Explore's backdrop (`assets/img/showroom-backdrop.webp`, the journey's gallery). The reference clips are not shown on the page. The Gemini clip `9f29a4ec` stays out (owner's instruction). `docs/storyboard.html` sets the reference videos beside the site scene by scene; `docs/wireframes.html` is the measured layout.
- Explore stage: one pinned stage, three families. Each family's real photographs sit in an arched window that wipes in and tilts in 3D with the pointer; the blurred photo fills the background.
- `src/specimens.js` (bundled with three.js to `assets/vendor/specimens.js` by `build.mjs`) + `assets/img/cutout/` + `assets/img/depth/`: the 3D specimens in Explore. Each family is a cut-out of SB Trading's own photograph (`tools/build-cutouts.py`: background removed with rembg's isnet model, leaf packs also by their green) made into geometry from that photograph's depth map (Depth Anything V2 Small), standing in a glass case on a pedestal with a glowing rim, with gold motes; it turns with scroll and pointer, then settles and stops drawing. A single photograph has no back, so it turns about a quarter-turn either way; full 360° objects need turntable photographs (`docs/SHOTLIST.md`). Loaded only when Explore is near; no WebGL, reduced motion or a lost context: the plain photograph in its arch. `assets/photodepth.js` remains for the Remotion film in `video/`.
- `assets/rifat.js` + `src/rifat-fn.js` → `functions/api/rifat.js`: Rifat, the enquiry assistant. With the Cloudflare Pages variable `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` set, the Pages Function answers with Claude and records enquiry fields; without it (or on a local server) Rifat runs as a labelled guided enquiry. The finished enquiry opens WhatsApp to SB Trading's number (+880 1878-707910, set in `data-whatsapp` on `<body>`) with the text filled in; it can also be copied.
- `assets/sound.js`: opt-in tactile sound, loaded only when the visitor presses Sound. Synthesised cues, no background bed: a hollow bamboo knock per family, a fibre brush on product hover, a soft chime when Rifat replies, a pluck when a message is sent.
- `assets/img/`: real product photographs; `sb-logo*.webp` and `sb-mark*.webp` are the original SB Trading logo with its white background removed (`-light` = the same mark knocked out to Cloud White for dark grounds; derived from the owner's file, awaiting approval).
- `assets/vendor/`: Motion 12, Lenis (bundled) and self-hosted fonts (Gloock, Schibsted Grotesk; OFL). No external requests from the page.
- Opening: the film itself, as in the owner's reference: darkness, then light shafts between 3D bamboo with gold dust drifting in them, the title, 3 families · 9 products · Bangladesh, Explore / Ask Rifat, and a scroll ring that fills as the film begins. Scrolling walks the camera forward through the grove into the lit showroom, past the bamboo splitting and the banana fibre winding into yarn, and ends at the gallery of three lit cases; in the showroom the captions, header and chapter bar turn to ink. Reduced motion shows the poster without the dust. Products open a spec drawer: a pane of white frosted glass with a brand-blue main button floating over the stage (right side on desktop, from the bottom on phones) with the photograph, a spec list (family, grade for graded yarns, supplied from Bangladesh; specification and minimum order "confirmed in your quotation" until SB Trading confirms them), "Request a quote" (the enquiry form opens in the drawer beside the product, then Send on WhatsApp there; it is the same form as Contact Us's), "Add to enquiry", "Ask Rifat about it" and the WhatsApp hand-off line. Explore is a bright showroom that stays inside the film's last room (the reference's gallery, softly out of focus behind each family): warm white walls, ink text, brand-blue accents, dimming to dusk as it leaves, with the header and chapter bar turning ink over it. In Explore each family's arch stands on a numbered plinth (01 / 03) with arrows to the families either side; added products collect in an enquiry tray that opens Rifat.
- The plain quotation form (under "Prefer a form?") works without Rifat and without JavaScript submission; it fills the same enquiry.
- `v1/`: the first single-file draft, kept unchanged.
- `DESIGN.md` (v1.0.0) + `.impeccable/design.json`: the design system recorded from the shipped code (28 semantic tokens, components and states, motion, type). `tests/tokens.mjs` fails the build if `styles.css :root` and DESIGN.md disagree.
- `docs/user-journey.md`: buyer journey (current vs desired) and gap analysis; assumptions until tested with real buyers.
- Build and test: `npm ci`, `npm run build`, `npx playwright install chromium`, `npm test` (171 checks: design tokens, copy and navigation, film sync, Rifat AI against a simulated API, the score rendered offline, caption contrast, accessibility with axe-core, and browser smoke on desktop and phone, including the 3D journey (dark grove to lit showroom, a different picture in every act, no video frames), the film label and the dust, the spec drawer, the quote form in the drawer, the exhibit plinths and the lit showroom). Set `CHROMIUM_PATH` where Playwright cannot install its own browser. GitHub Actions runs the same on pull requests and pushes to `main`; it does not deploy.
- Operational notes, open items and the Access decision gate: `HANDOFF_CODEX.md`.
- Not used from the AI drafts: invented specifications, certifications and pricing. Add them only after SB Trading confirms.
- `video/`: the Remotion brand film (16 s with sound, 16:9 and 9:16), built from the site's design system, photographs, depth renderer and score. `cd video && npm install && npx playwright install chromium && npm run render`. Rendering syncs colours and assets from the site (`sync-design.mjs`) and renders the soundtrack (`render-score.mjs`, needs Playwright's Chromium and ffmpeg). In a sandbox without downloads, set `CHROMIUM_PATH` (and `REMOTION_BROWSER` for Remotion's headless shell) to installed browsers instead. `tests/film-sync.mjs` fails if the film drifts from DESIGN.md or the site's copy.
