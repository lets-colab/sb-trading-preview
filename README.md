# sb-trading-preview
SB Trading — From leaf to fibre | client review draft (non-production, `noindex`).

- `index.html`, `styles.css`, `site.js`: one continuous blue-hour scroll story in five stages (Arrive → Discover → Transition → Explore → Enquire), with a chapter bar along the bottom.
- `assets/film/{d,m}/`: 217 WebP frames scrubbed by scroll (desktop 1280×720, phone 405×720 crop), built by `tools/build-film.py` from the owner's four reference clips (generated footage), cut into one continuous journey: the bamboo grove in the first light opening onto the pavilion (97f87dc2, 0.25-1.95 s); the showroom's three glass cases, bamboo, banana fibre yarn and banana bark (97f87dc2, 6.1-8.1 s); the specimen in a cold beam in a steel-blue hall, splitting open and its fibres swirling into yarn (6daa857f, 0.4-4.3 s); the yarn and a banana bark sheet in cyan and gold light (43a4edb4, 13-15.6 s); pulling back to the whole range under a daylit glass dome (fa9b5fd6, 10.35-13.2 s). No join is a cross-fade: a bamboo culm passes close in front of the lens into the showroom, a glint sweeps across the glass as the camera pushes into the hall, the yarn is matched from shot to shot, and the dark around it opens onto the dome. The clips' drawn labels and cards are blurred out. The same build cuts the clips' own audio exactly as the picture (`assets/film/sound.mp3`, each frame's time in `film.json`) and loops their steady shimmer (`assets/film/bed.mp3`). With motion the film is a journey after hatom.com: a gate in live 3D, then six chapters the camera flies between on a click (the grove, the showroom, inside the material, fibre to yarn, bark and leaves, the whole range), each narrated (`assets/voice/ch-*.mp3`, one line that describes what is on screen) with one subtitle line. Each family's ring appears once, on its own glass case in the showroom: tap for its story, hold for its products on a glass board. The content map, one home per fact, is `docs/content-map.md`.
- Explore stage: one pinned stage, three families. Each family's real photographs sit in an arched window that wipes in and tilts in 3D with the pointer; the blurred photo fills the background.
- `src/specimens.js` (bundled with three.js to `assets/vendor/specimens.js` by `build.mjs`) + `assets/img/cutout/` + `assets/img/depth/`: the 3D specimens in Explore. Each family is a cut-out of SB Trading's own photograph (`tools/build-cutouts.py`: background removed with rembg's isnet model, leaf packs also by their green) made into geometry from that photograph's depth map (Depth Anything V2 Small), standing in a glass case on a pedestal with a glowing rim, with gold motes; it turns with scroll and pointer, then settles and stops drawing. A single photograph has no back, so it turns about a quarter-turn either way; full 360° objects need turntable photographs (`docs/SHOTLIST.md`). Loaded only when Explore is near; no WebGL, reduced motion or a lost context: the plain photograph in its arch. `assets/photodepth.js` remains for the Remotion film in `video/`.
- `assets/rifat.js` + `src/rifat-fn.js` → `functions/api/rifat.js`: Rifat, the enquiry assistant, shown as the owner's portrait of Rifat (`assets/img/rifat/`, cut out with rembg) standing in a cyan ring at the end of the journey, always labelled AI; his face sits in the chat bar. With the Cloudflare Pages variable `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` set, the Pages Function answers with Claude and records enquiry fields; without it (or on a local server) Rifat runs as a labelled guided enquiry. Knowledge: `assets/rifat-knowledge.js` (what the site states about SB Trading, ordering, contact, and the materials' history) is what Rifat answers questions from, in both modes: Claude reads it through a `search_knowledge` tool; guided mode answers a question from it, then carries on with the enquiry. Optionally, with `SUPABASE_URL` and `SUPABASE_ANON_KEY` on the Pages project, a Supabase table `rifat_knowledge` (schema and seed: `docs/supabase/rifat_knowledge.sql`, read-only for the anon role) is searched too, so confirmed facts can be added without a redeploy. The finished enquiry opens WhatsApp to SB Trading's number (+880 1878-707910, set in `data-whatsapp` on `<body>`) with the text filled in; it can also be copied. The narrator's recordings are in `assets/voice/`: six chapter lines, three family stories and three hold-for-details lines, in Indian English voices: the three family stories by the ElevenLabs storyteller "Moksh" (recorded on the ElevenLabs account; the bamboo story ends at "...from the same roots"), the chapter and hold-for-details lines by Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0, voice "Psi", Indian English male), recorded locally by `tools/narrate.py` with no account or key, which also checks each recording against its script with Whisper. Once ElevenLabs recording is available again, the nine short lines can be re-recorded in Moksh so one voice speaks throughout.
- `assets/sound.js`: opt-in sound, loaded only when the visitor presses Sound. The score is generated in the browser, slow and South Asian, made for headphones: a tanpura drone (Pa Sa Sa Sa, with the jawari shimmer), sparse santoor strokes and bansuri phrases in raga Bhupali on C, the key of the owner's film clips, with birds, close bamboo leaves and wind placed around the listener; the chapter sets the blend. When the film's own sound has loaded, the clips' shimmer joins it as a layer, each camera flight plays the soundtrack of exactly the frames it crosses, and a moment crossed by scrolling plays its own slice; until then, or without it, the film's moments are generated (leaves, the culm brushing past, glass, the glint, the beam, the split, the fibres, the yarn, the bark, the dome). Tactile cues: a bamboo knock per family, a light santoor touch on hover, temple-bell notes when Rifat replies, a pluck on send. `tests/score.mjs` renders it offline and measures it, and checks the film soundtrack and its frame map. Narration: Indian English, the storyteller Moksh for the family stories and Kokoro's Psi for the chapter lines (see Rifat's entry above).
- `assets/img/`: real product photographs; `sb-logo*.webp` and `sb-mark*.webp` are the original SB Trading logo with its white background removed (`-light` = the same mark knocked out to Cloud White for dark grounds; derived from the owner's file, awaiting approval).
- `assets/vendor/`: Motion 12, Lenis (bundled) and self-hosted fonts (Gloock, Schibsted Grotesk; OFL). No external requests from the page.
- Opening: the film itself, as in the owner's reference: darkness, then light shafts between bamboo with seeded gold dust drifting in them, the title, 3 families · 9 products · Bangladesh, Explore / Ask Rifat, and a scroll ring that fills as the film begins. Scrolling walks forward through the grove into the light, through the fibres, and ends in the gallery of three lit cases; each caption has its own soft scrim so the footage keeps its light. Reduced motion shows a poster without the dust. Products open a spec drawer: a pane of white frosted glass with a brand-blue main button floating over the stage (right side on desktop, from the bottom on phones) with the photograph, a spec list (family, grade for graded yarns, supplied from Bangladesh; specification and minimum order "confirmed in your quotation" until SB Trading confirms them), "Request a quote" (the enquiry form opens in the drawer beside the product, then Send on WhatsApp there; it is the same form as Contact Us's), "Add to enquiry", "Ask Rifat about it" and the WhatsApp hand-off line. Explore is a bright showroom that stays inside the film's last room (the reference's gallery, softly out of focus behind each family): warm white walls, ink text, brand-blue accents, dimming to dusk as it leaves, with the header and chapter bar turning ink over it. In Explore each family's arch stands on a numbered plinth (01 / 03) with arrows to the families either side; added products collect in an enquiry tray that opens Rifat.
- Rifat's free AI on GitHub Pages: `src/rifat-gemini.js` → `supabase/functions/rifat/` (Supabase Edge Function, Google Gemini free tier; same rules and knowledge as the Claude endpoint, from `src/rifat-core.js`). The page calls whatever address is in `data-rifat-endpoint` on `<body>`; empty means `api/rifat` (Cloudflare), which GitHub Pages does not have, so Rifat runs in guided mode there until the owner sets it up:
  1. Create a free Supabase project; in its SQL editor run `docs/supabase/rifat_knowledge.sql`.
  2. Create a free Gemini API key in Google AI Studio and confirm the current free Flash model name.
  3. With the Supabase CLI: `supabase secrets set GEMINI_API_KEY=<key>` (and `GEMINI_MODEL=<name>` if it is not `gemini-3.6-flash`), then `supabase functions deploy rifat --no-verify-jwt`. Keys go only into Supabase, never into the repo or a chat.
  4. Put the function's address (`https://<project>.supabase.co/functions/v1/rifat`) in `data-rifat-endpoint`; Rifat's label then reads "AI assistant · online · Google Gemini (free tier)" with the privacy note (on the free tier, outside the EEA, Switzerland and the UK, Google may use messages to improve its models; a paid key removes that).
  `tests/rifat-gemini.mjs` proves the function against a simulated Gemini API.
- The plain quotation form (under "Prefer a form?") works without Rifat and without JavaScript submission; it fills the same enquiry.
- `v1/`: the first single-file draft, kept unchanged.
- `DESIGN.md` (v1.0.0) + `.impeccable/design.json`: the design system recorded from the shipped code (28 semantic tokens, components and states, motion, type). `tests/tokens.mjs` fails the build if `styles.css :root` and DESIGN.md disagree.
- `docs/user-journey.md`: buyer journey (current vs desired) and gap analysis; assumptions until tested with real buyers.
- Version tags: every file the page loads is named with `?v=` and a hash of its contents, so a returning visitor's browser can never pair the new page with an old stylesheet or old film frames. After changing any file the page loads (a stylesheet, script, photograph, narration or the film), run `node tools/stamp.mjs`; `npm run build` runs it too, and `npm test` fails while a tag is stale. A new file is named once by hand as `path?v=00000000`, and the stamp fills it in.
- Build and test: `npm ci`, `npm run build`, `npx playwright install chromium`, `npm test` (336 checks: design tokens, copy and navigation, film sync, Rifat AI against a simulated API, the score rendered offline, caption contrast, accessibility with axe-core, version tags (every file fetched on a desktop and a phone journey carries its current tag), contrast of every line over the film (gate, subtitles, rings, edge labels), and browser smoke on desktop and phone, including the film label and the dust, the spec drawer, the quote form in the drawer, the exhibit plinths and the lit showroom). Set `CHROMIUM_PATH` where Playwright cannot install its own browser. GitHub Actions runs the same on pull requests and pushes to `main`; it does not deploy.
- Operational notes, open items and the Access decision gate: `HANDOFF_CODEX.md`.
- Not used from the AI drafts: invented specifications, certifications and pricing. Add them only after SB Trading confirms.
- `video/`: the Remotion brand film (16 s with sound, 16:9 and 9:16), built from the site's design system, photographs, depth renderer and score. `cd video && npm install && npx playwright install chromium && npm run render`. Rendering syncs colours and assets from the site (`sync-design.mjs`) and renders the soundtrack (`render-score.mjs`, needs Playwright's Chromium and ffmpeg). In a sandbox without downloads, set `CHROMIUM_PATH` (and `REMOTION_BROWSER` for Remotion's headless shell) to installed browsers instead. `tests/film-sync.mjs` fails if the film drifts from DESIGN.md or the site's copy.
