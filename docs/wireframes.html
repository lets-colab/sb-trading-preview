Frame 4 · 04 Explore

A family on stage

A bright showroom straight after the film’s gallery: warm white walls, ink text, brand-blue accents; it dims to dusk as it leaves. One pinned stage, three families in turn. Text and product chips on the left; on the right, an arch window with the family’s photograph drawn in depth (it tilts with the pointer). Under the arch, a plinth carries the family’s number (01 / 03), its name and arrows to the families either side. On phones the arch and plinth sit above the text.

Frame 5 · 04 Explore

The spec drawer

A product chip opens a pane of frosted glass that floats clear of the edges (from the right on desktop, from the bottom on phones), with the family still visible behind it. Photograph, name, spec list, Request a quote (the main action) and Add to enquiry, Ask Rifat about it, and the hand-off line. Request a quote turns the drawer into the enquiry form beside the same product, then offers Send on WhatsApp there. Each product has its own shareable address.

Frame 7 · 05 Enquire

Rifat and the enquiry

Rifat, an AI assistant labelled as AI, asks for the missing details one at a time, with suggested answers. Beside it (below on phones), “Your enquiry” fills in as you answer; the buyer sends it on WhatsApp. A plain form is offered for anyone who prefers it.
