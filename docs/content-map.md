and, where a visitor can skip the film, one on the direct path. A change to a fact is made in its home; nothing else
restates it.

## Deliberate second homes (a visitor on the direct path)

- **The stories:** "Hear its story" in Explore, only when motion is turned off (the film is then a still poster with no
  rings). With motion they are on the showroom's rings only, so a visitor never meets the same story twice.
- **"Supplied from Bangladesh"** in the spec drawer: a field of the product's specification, not narration.

## Removed (17 Nov 2026)

The opening's figures (3 families, 9 products, Bangladesh), the list in the opening line, "Three families, nine products"
in the specimen chapter and in About Us, the end chapter's family cards (Explore follows at once), the product notes that
repeated the product buttons under them, "from Bangladesh" and "confirmed with your quotation" in every glass board,
chapter headings that repeated their narration word for word, the same three rings in four chapters, and the chapter bar's
"Arrive / Discover / Transition" (now the journey's stops: Grove, Showroom, Inside, Explore, Enquire).
