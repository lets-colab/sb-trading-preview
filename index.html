How ordering works.

  1. Tell us what you need.

    The product, the quantity and where it should be delivered. Rifat helps you put it together.

  2. Receive a quotation.

    SB Trading's team replies with price, specification and shipping options.

  3. Confirm samples or the order.

    Agree the details with the team before anything is shipped.

Still to be confirmed by SB Trading: minimum order quantities, origin and export ports, shipping terms, lead times and certifications.
